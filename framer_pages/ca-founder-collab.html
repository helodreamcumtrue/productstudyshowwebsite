RAZORPAYX PAYROLL

RAZORPAYX PAYROLL

CA & Founder Collaboration

Project Type

Motion Graphics, Script Writing

Target Audience

Startup Founders and Chartered Accountants

Timeframe

2 Weeks

Problem

Many Founders and Chartered Accountants are unaware of the inefficiencies and challenges in their current collaboration process (If they are aware, they believe there is no better method available). Traditional practices often lead to miscommunication, incomplete data sharing, and administrative bottlenecks.    

This results in missed opportunities for improvement and many founders spend more time managing financial data and working through administrative issues with their Chartered Accountants than they do focusing on strategic financial planning.

Objectives

The objective of this project is to create awareness on how traditional collaboration methods between founders and Chartered Accountants divert valuable time away from strategic financial discussions and how this can be solved with RazorpayX.

The Output

Roles & Responsibilities

As the sole designer on the project, I collaborated with a PMM

Research

I did an extensive research activity to understand different kinds of product videos & styles.

Product understanding

Collaborated with product & marketing teams to better understand & depict the product accurately in the video.

Script Writing

Upon identifying shortcomings in the script, I revised it to enhance the story’s impact and appeal to users.

Storyboarding & motion graphics

Solely designed the storyboard & the motion graphic video

Script Development

The script provided by the marketing team was reviewed and I found to be overly sales-focused and lacking a compelling storyline.

To address this, I re-wrote the script to be more engaging, friendly, and user-centric. Here is a small snippet

Initial script

It’s the month-end. And your chartered accountant needs data. A lot of data. What if we told you that you don’t have to be scared of talking to your CA?
  Now give them secure, self-serve access to everything they need

From Reports, Transactions, Invoices,To Challans
Right from the dashboard with all the context they need to close your books faster.

How do we make this more engaging?

Your chartered accountant needs info.
A lot of info.
Like seriously, too much info.
They need invoices, challans, reports, transactions,
bills for payments you don’t remember making.
Oh and did we mention. A bazillion* invoices. (*Approx figure)

Well this is how we fixed that!

Bring in humour to the story

Make the solution stage clear

The revised script received immediate approval from stakeholders, with no further changes required. Once alignment was achieved, I began work on the storyboard.

A detailed storyboard was created to map out the video’s flow and ensure each segment communicated key points effectively. This phase included developing visual representations of each scene and planning the animation sequences.

Design & Animation Execution

First a broad exploration was done to evaluate several design options to ensure the chosen style not only reflected contemporary trends but also effectively conveyed the message with precision and clarity.

Various visual styles were explored before settling on a final approach that merged modern aesthetics with clear, impactful visuals.

Next, the visuals for the entire storyboard were created, and animation work began to bring them to life.

The Output

Results

The video received significantly higher numbers compared to industry benchmark.

Effective Communication: Successfully communicated the challenges in traditional collaboration and showcased RazorpayX as a solution, resonating well with the target audience.

34.40%

Engagement rate

5.6X Industry benchmark

Conclusion

With solid research, smart scriptwriting, and top-notch animation, the video tackled collaboration challenges and positioned RazorpayX as a key solution. The high engagement rate proves it succeeded in grabbing and keeping the audience’s attention.

This project demonstrated the power of thoughtfully designed motion graphics in communicating complex ideas.
