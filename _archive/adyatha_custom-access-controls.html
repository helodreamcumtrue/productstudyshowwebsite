& Approval Workflows

Project Type

Motion Graphics, Script Writing

Target Audience

Founders & Finance Managers

Timeframe

1.5 Weeks

Problem

Startups often struggle with managing and delegating financial tasks effectively due to concerns about privacy, control, and security.

The lack of customisable access and approval workflows can lead to inefficiencies and potential risks, making it challenging to ensure that only the right people have access to sensitive financial information and that approvals are in place.

Objectives

The project aims to showcase RazorpayX's new custom access controls and approval workflows, illustrating how these features help startups delegate tasks confidently, boost collaboration, and ensure top-notch security and control without extra costs.

The video is required to generate excitement on social media, driving awareness and interest in RazorpayX’s innovative banking solutions.

Script Development

The initial script provided was overly technical and failed to resonate with the audience. Recognizing this, I proposed a narrative shift to focus on the practical challenges faced by startups.


I included real-world examples by sourcing tweets from startup founders. This revision not only improved the narrative but also laid the groundwork for a targeted social media campaign that leveraged these insights.

This simple addition, allowed us to tag different founders on social media and leverage their audiences to increase engagement on our page

Design & Animation Execution

Developed a storyboard that introduced a novel storytelling technique using abstract shapes to represent different aspects of the product. This creative direction was well-received and required minimal revisions, showcasing the clarity and effectiveness of the concept.  


The animation focus was to add a dynamic and engaging elements to the video and to keep it fast and peppy.
