And now I feel I need to be a lot more interesting *sweats* Here’s the rundown: I’m a Senior Communication Designer with 4 years of experience at Razorpay. I’m obsessed with stories—telling them, listening to them, reading them, and of course, crafting them. My love for stories probably ties into the fact that I’m a huge history nerd (seriously, ask me about Henry VIII and his many wives). I believe that weaving stories into business goals and strategy is crucial for creating designs that truly connect with users. After all, no matter who your audience is, they experience emotions. Outside of envying the life of my very spoilt cat, I'm passionate about the value of education and design thinking. I've twice had the privilege of serving as an External Jury member for the Visual Communication Design department at Jain University School of Design, Media, and Creative Arts. In a nutshell, I'm all about blending creativity with purpose, ensuring every design I create not only looks great but also tells a story that resonates. Whether it's through crafting compelling visuals, diving into historical rabbit holes, or mentoring future designers, I’m constantly striving to make an impact.
