Problem Statement
PNB's banking website is difficult to navigate, with complex menus and a cluttered homepage 
that frustrate users trying to complete tasks like fund transfers, bill payments, and account 
management. The lack of personalization and outdated design further complicates the user 
experience, especially for mobile users. These issues lead to inefficiency, user frustration, and a 
negative impact on customer retention.

This case study seeks to improve the website’s usability by addressing navigation, information 
overload, and visual design, ultimately enhancing the overall user experience.

CONCLUSION
We aimed to understand and improve the Instagram Reels experience to drive higher
engagement and user retention, focusing on content creation and discovery. After a
thorough product teardown, we identified critical areas for growth.
Problems Identified:
1. High friction for new creators to produce high-quality short-form video content.
2. Limited discovery of original, niche content outside of viral trends.
3. Lack of clear monetization pathways for emerging Reels creators.
Solutions:
1. Enhanced Reels Creation Suite with Al-powered editing tools and templates.
2. Integrated 'Creators Fund' and better algorithmic matching for niche content discovery.
