This is me!

Hello! Welcome to my design space.

If you have landed here, you are probably curious to learn about the mind behind the products, teardowns, and design systems.

Here's the rundown: I'm Lakshay Jain, a Product Designer & UI/UX Strategist crafting intuitive digital products, accessible fintech experiences, and data-driven systems.

I'm obsessed with transforming complex user problems into crisp, functional blueprints. From reimagining digital banking with Punjab National Bank, to dissecting onboarding mechanics for Discord and Instagram Reels, to engineering sustainable delivery systems for Domino's—I believe great products emerge where deep user empathy meets engineering precision.

I treat design not as decoration, but as product strategy. Every component hierarchy, typography scale, and interaction curve is an intentional lever to eliminate user friction, establish institutional trust, and drive measurable product adoption.

Beyond Figma and systems architecture, I'm deeply passionate about developer communities and product innovation. I actively contribute to campus tech initiatives, mentor peers in design thinking, and build open-source tools with Python, React, and Supabase.

I lead design and technical efforts for hackathons, campus events, and workflow automation tools—focusing on WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, fluid micro-interactions, and scalable design tokens.

In a nutshell, I blend aesthetic craftsmanship with engineering clarity—ensuring every digital product not only looks stunning, but delivers measurable results and delightful user interactions.
