Project Type

Product Strategy, UX Research, FTUX & Retention

Target Audience

Novice Discord Users, Students & Casual Gamers

Timeframe

3 Weeks

Problem

Discord has 150M+ MAU and over 4 billion minutes of daily conversation, yet suffers ~60% Day-1 drop-off among non-gamers. 'The Empty Hallway' effect causes 4 out of 5 first-time users to abandon a server within 10 minutes without sending a single message due to cognitive overload, bot notification spam, and complex server layouts.

Objectives

The objective of this product teardown is to design a frictionless First-Time User Experience (FTUX) that guides users to their first message in under 60 seconds, demystifies channel architecture, and boosts Day-1 retention by 20% using the RICE prioritization framework.

Teardown Frameworks

Roles & Responsibilities

As the product designer & strategist, I conducted user interviews, mapped friction points, applied RICE prioritization, and designed high-fidelity UI solutions.

Layout & Structuring

Deconstructed the first-time user journey and mapped friction points causing ~60% Day-1 drop-off.

Generated designs with AI

Synthesized the persona of Harshit Aggarwal (19, Casual Gamer & Student) and evaluated drop-off moments.

Brainstorming sessions

Scored interventions using the RICE prioritization framework (Contextual Micro-Guides RICE 900, Role-Based Tours RICE 600).

Collaboration with marketing

Designed high-fidelity mobile and desktop prototypes eliminating the 'Empty Hallway' effect.

Design approach

Through user interviews with students and casual gamers like Harshit Aggarwal (19), I mapped drop-off moments across the initial onboarding funnel. I then broke down solutions into key strategic objectives:

Quick Win: Contextual Micro-Guides (RICE 900)

Quick Win: Contextual Tooltips (RICE 900) - Lightweight micro-guides explaining channels, voice rooms, and bots in-line without cognitive overload.

Major Project: Role-Based Onboarding (RICE 600)

Introduce customizable role selectors ('I'm a Designer', 'I'm a Developer', 'Just Browsing') that curate relevant channels and silence notification clutter.

Interest-Based Guided Discovery

Deliver a report that not only delights existing customers but also serves as an effective research tool for new clients, helping them navigate salary trends and hiring patterns.

Typography & layout explorations

I experimented with various layout structures and typesettings to find the most effective way to present the data. This involved creating multiple iterations to refine the layout for clarity and visual appeal.

Framework Analysis: RICE Prioritization

Style Experimentation: To best showcase the data, I explored several visual styles including 3D effects, paper cutouts, and human imagery. Each style was evaluated for its ability to enhance data presentation and engage readers.

Final Style Choice: After thorough experimentation, I settled on a visual style that blended modern aesthetics with clear data representation, ensuring the report was both attractive and informative.

The process involved constant reiteration to ensure that all spreads were, interesting and contextual to the report
