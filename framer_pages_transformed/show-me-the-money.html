Revenue engine alongside the core product primitives powering every community.

04 — Diagnosis

The problem: the Day-1 drop-off

Why new users bounce before their first real conversation — driving a ~60% Day-1 drop-off.

01 · Empty State

The empty hallway

Users sign up and immediately hit a blank screen with zero prompts on where to go next.

02 · Jargon

Cognitive load

Unfamiliar terms like "servers," "channels," "roles" and "bots" scare off non-technical newcomers.

03 · One-Size-Fits-All

Generic welcome

Every user gets the same landing experience whether they joined for gaming, study groups or art.

Key insight

4 out of 5 new users leave the app within their first 10 minutes if they don't join an active server.

05 — Experience Map

The new user journey

Emotions dip sharply after sign-up until the user joins their first channel — right where onboarding guidance is missing.

1. Discovery🤔
User goals

Find out what Discord is and download it.

Touchpoints

Friend's invite link, YouTube ads, App Store.

Pain points

Unclear value proposition for non-gamers.

Opportunities

Clear messaging showcasing diverse interest groups.

2. Sign up😐
User goals

Create an account quickly.

Touchpoints

Sign-up form, email verification, captcha.

Pain points

Friction from strict password and captcha steps.

Opportunities

Simplified phone sign-up and SSO options.

3. First join😟
User goals

Understand what to do inside a server.

Touchpoints

Main dashboard, sidebar channels, welcome bot.

Pain points

Overwhelming UI, too many bots, confusing jargon.

Opportunities

Interactive walkthroughs highlighting basic controls.

4. Engagement😊
User goals

Have a first conversation or voice call.

Touchpoints

Voice chat, reactions, direct messaging.

Pain points

Social anxiety about speaking up in public rooms.

Opportunities

Guided icebreakers and low-pressure intro rooms.

07 — Proposed Solutions

Solution 1: interactive guided tours

Replace "empty state" confusion with a structured, role-based setup wizard.

The friction

New users face cognitive overload from endless channels and unread badges the moment they join.

The fix

An interactive overlay highlights key areas and suppresses notifications until the user completes setup.

+15%Projected retention
2.5xSetup speed
"Pick your role" shows only the channels relevant to each person's interests.

New users don't need more options. They need a clear first step.

08 — Onboarding Discovery

Solution 2: guided discovery, curating the chaos

Help users find their tribe in three clicks or fewer, reducing empty-state anxiety.

Goal

Immediate value

Reduce new-user churn by giving immediate value and connection points at sign-up.

The problem

Choice overload

"Empty state anxiety" and choice overload from thousands of unorganized servers.

The fix

Curated setup

A guided flow that curates server suggestions based on user interests.

A three-step flow: pick interests, see a curated feed, join in one click.
Step 01

Interest selection

Users select tags such as gaming, music and tech.

Step 02

Curated feed

An algorithm suggests the top three communities.

Step 03

Instant join

One-click entry into active channels.

09 — Summary

Conclusion and projected impact

The friction points, the interventions, and the projected growth for Discord's new-user experience.

Root Causes

Key problems

  • Analysis paralysis: new users abandon the app when faced with 50+ server categories right after sign-up
  • Empty state confusion: landing in empty servers with no guidance feels like a dead end
Interventions

Proposed solutions

  • Gamified onboarding: an interactive tutorial that rewards users with badges for completing profile setup
  • Starter servers: pre-populated, bot-guided servers that make the first "safe" interaction easy
Outcome

Projected impact

Turning confusion into clear guidance within the critical first 10 minutes.

+20%Projected lift in Day-1 retention

If a new user's first ten minutes feel welcoming, they are far more likely to stay and join in.
