01 / Landscape & Scale

Product Overview

Launched by Instagram in 2020, Reels is a short-form video feature designed to compete with platforms like TikTok, letting users create and discover entertaining videos up to 90 seconds long.

Sitting directly inside the Instagram app, Reels taps into a massive existing user base with audio, AR effects, editing tools, a dedicated discovery feed, and creator monetization—making it central to Instagram's engagement and growth strategy.

She keeps seeing the same trends and sounds, and wants more variety that truly fits her niche interests.

03 / Problem Framing

Improving Content Discovery & Engagement

The core problem to solve is to improve Reels content discovery so that engagement rises, anchored on watch time, creator follows, and share frequency. To move any of these metrics, users have to discover more relevant content or connect with more creators.

Better discovery means showing people more relevant content and helping them find the creators who fit them.

Engagement = Watch Time × Creator Follows × No. of Sessions
Watch TimeRelevance keeps viewers watching past the first 3 seconds.
Creator FollowsNiche discovery turns passive scrollers into loyal followers.
Share FrequencyCo-created & memorable clips drive organic social sharing.

06 / Proposed Solutions

Solution 1: Enhanced Editing Tools

Give creators pro-level editing directly inside the Reels camera—reducing off-platform friction and raising content quality. Users can trim, splice, add advanced effects, and mix multi-track audio before publishing.

  1. Reels camera
  2. Select media & record
  3. Open Editing Studio
  4. Timeline editing
  5. Effects & audio mix
  6. Preview & adjust
  7. Captions & share

Entry directly from the Reels camera into an intuitive timeline with trimming, multi-track audio, and effect overlays, followed by final preview and captioning.

Creators want to make something good without learning a whole editing suite.

Co-Creation & Reach

Solution 2: Collaborative Reels

Multiple users contribute clips to a single shared Reel. Creators invite friends, combine clips, and publish the final Reel to both profiles—widening organic reach and encouraging participation.

  1. Open Reels camera
  2. Tap Collaborate
  3. Invite user
  4. User accepts
  5. Both record & add clips
  6. Edit together
  7. Post to both profiles

Left: inviting a collaborator during Reel creation. Right: the finished Reel publishes to both profiles with shared authorship.

08 / Wrap-Up

Conclusion

This teardown set out to improve the Reels experience to drive stronger engagement and retention across both content creation and discovery.

Problems Identified

  • High friction for new creators to produce high-quality short-form video
  • Limited discovery of original, niche content outside viral trends
  • No clear monetization path for emerging Reels creators

Solutions Proposed

  • An enhanced Reels creation suite with AI-powered editing tools and templates
  • An integrated Creators Fund and sharper algorithmic matching for niche discovery

Fixing discovery and lowering the barrier to creating gives viewers more to love and gives creators a reason to keep posting.
