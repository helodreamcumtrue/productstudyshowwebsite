PRODUCT TEARDOWN

Domino's
Packaging Redesign

Project Type

GTM Strategy, Motion design & Print Design

Target Audience

Eco-Conscious Consumers, Operators & Delivery Fleets

Timeframe

3 Weeks

Problem

Why the legacy pizza box failed: 1) Thermodynamics - 40% heat loss in 15 minutes due to standard uninsulated corrugated board; 2) Sustainability First - 30% rendered unrecyclable from oil grease saturation; 3) Courier Structural Failure - weak sidewalls crushed when stacking > 3 boxes on delivery bikes.

Objectives

Deconstructing the strategic pivot to eco-friendly, thermally efficient, stackable packaging designed for the modern delivery ecosystem across 1,500+ franchise locations.

Persona Synthesis: Aman Kapoor (Junior Developer, Metro India) orders late-night food 3x/week to fuel coding sessions. He values piping-hot food, but actively avoids single-use plastic waste.

The Output

Packaging Schematics & Thermal Models

Roles & Responsibilities

As product strategist and systems designer, I deconstructed thermodynamic heat transfer, evaluated supply chain economics, and designed user-friendly unboxing workflows.

Set the creative direction

Engineered around 4 core business pillars: 1) Sustainability First (-30% plastic); 2) Heat Retention (+15% longer thermal holding); 3) Courier Logistics (2x bag density); 4) Unboxing UX.

Storyboarding & visuals

I collaborated closely with my peers to brainstorm & finalise on the storyboard & final set of visuals.

Animation

Worked solely on the animations of all frames which included motion graphics, character rigging, etc.

Project management

Setting appropriate milestones, keeping a regular & open communication with the cross-functional teams

4 Strategic Business Pillars

RazorpayX Payroll initially served small and medium businesses but has since evolved to meet the needs of enterprises. With enterprises being a distinct target segment, the goal was to position RazorpayX Payroll as the leading solution for them. To make the launch more engaging, CXOs were encouraged to assess their payroll automation level & see which processes they could improve using an assessment.

Storyboard & Visual Style

Each team member developed their own interpretation and approach for weaving the story through visuals.

A mix and match of different ideas and concepts was selected and I created a singular storyboard, which was presented to the stakeholders.

After aligning on the approach and the concepts behind the different frames of the story, three key frames were selected to explore various visual directions.

A consensus was reached on the most effective style to pursue. And the entire storyboard was developed to align with the selected visual approach, ensuring a cohesive presentation.

....and then it was time to tackle the next phase: Animation.

With the storyboard complete, it was time to switch gears to video creation. Assets were organized and moved to After Effects.

Creating the animations included multiple caveats & nuances of motion. The animation process included advanced techniques, such as character rigging, to add a dynamic and engaging element to the video. This approach was a significant departure from previous projects and set a new standard for motion design within the company.

The Output

Many enterprises in India are seeking advanced payroll solutions and a panel discussion featuring CHROs from enterprise organizations would effectively highlight the new positioning for RazorpayX Payroll.

Deconstructing operational and thermodynamic logistics across Domino's 1,500+ franchise delivery ecosystem: solving pizza sogginess, box collapse, and single-use plastic waste.

The Team

Delivering high-class output and seeing them to success would not be possible as a one woman team. This project was a collaborative effort involving the marketing, product, and design teams. While I led the animation, storytelling, visual aesthetics and project management, I worked closely with marketing stakeholders to ensure our storyline and overarching brand vision stood out. I also guided my fellow designers on the visual look and design language, ensuring all collaterals looked cohesive and unified.

Outcome & Strategic Summary

The project wrapped up with a great event where the video and assets got positive feedback from everyone who attended. The engagement metrics were impressive too.

This experience highlighted how important it is to stay flexible and keep learning in a fast-paced creative setting. It also showed how crucial good communication and teamwork are for hitting project goals. Overall, this project was a big milestone for me, proving I can lead, innovate, and deliver great creative solutions.
