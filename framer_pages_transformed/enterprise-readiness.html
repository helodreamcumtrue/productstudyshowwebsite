01 / Why The Old Box Failed

Problem: Identifying the Friction

The standard pizza box created friction in three places: it let pizza go cold, it couldn't be recycled once greasy, and it didn't hold up when stacked during rush deliveries.

Critical

Thermodynamics

Standard corrugated cardboard has no insulation, so pizza loses its ideal serving temperature rapidly during delivery.

40%Heat loss in 15 minutes
High Waste

Sustainability

Grease soaks into the fibres, rendering a large share of legacy boxes unrecyclable in municipal facilities.

30%Unrecyclable volume
Friction

Structural Integrity

Weak sidewalls limit stacking during bulk deliveries, often crushing toppings and hurting presentation.

3 boxesSafe stack limit
The Core Insight

The market needed a solution that strictly balances thermal efficiency with eco-accountability.

02 / Business Objectives

Strategic Rationale: Why the Change?

Shifting to eco-friendly, stackable packaging built for the modern delivery ecosystem rests on four strategic objectives aimed at long-term growth.

-30% plastic

Premium

Brand modernization

Lift brand perception from "fast food" to a "premium dining experience".

+15% heat

Heat retention

A new corrugated structure keeps heat 15% longer during last-mile delivery.

2x efficiency

Stackability

Optimized geometry fits standard courier bags and doubles capacity.

05 / Execution

From Concept to Counter: The Rollout

A phased rollout bringing the new packaging to 1,500+ franchise locations. Phase 2 is currently active.

  1. Phase 1: Pilot program

    Completed Q1 2024

    Beta tested in 50 select stores across Tier-1 metro cities (Delhi and Mumbai pilots), focused on customer feedback about heat retention.

    50 stores98% positive feedback

  2. Phase 2: Supply chain optimization

    In progress Q2 2024

    Upcoming Q3 2024
